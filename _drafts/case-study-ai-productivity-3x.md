## CASE STUDY PLAN

**This is a new case study, not an expansion of an existing post.**

**Why This Matters:**
- You claim 3X productivity with AI tools
- This is a differentiated skill that employers value
- But the claim needs proof to be credible
- Most people either resist AI or use it superficially

---

## ELEMENTS TO DOCUMENT

### The Baseline (Before AI-Assisted Development)

- What was your typical feature delivery cadence?
- How did you spend your time? (Coding, debugging, research, meetings, etc.)
- What were your bottlenecks?
- Pick 2-3 representative features and document: scope, time to complete, quality

### The Transition

- When did you start using AI tools seriously?
- What tools? (Claude, Copilot, etc.)
- What was the learning curve?
- Early mistakes: what didn't work?
- When did it start feeling natural?

### The After State

- Same 2-3 representative features (similar scope): time to complete, quality
- Where does AI help most? (Boilerplate, debugging, research, design?)
- Where does AI NOT help? (What still requires human judgment?)
- How has your role changed? (More design, less typing?)

### The Metrics

Be specific:
- Features per sprint/month: before vs. after
- Bug rates: before vs. after
- Time on different activities: before vs. after
- Code review feedback: any change in quality perception?

### The Nuance

What makes this credible:
- What AI can't do (judgment, architecture, stakeholder alignment)
- Where AI makes you faster vs. where it just shifts work
- The meta-skill: knowing when to use AI vs. when to think yourself
- Risks: atrophy concerns and how you address them

---

## STRUCTURE

1. **The Skeptic's Starting Point** — Initial doubts about AI tools
2. **The Experiment** — How you started integrating AI
3. **The Learning Curve** — What didn't work, what clicked
4. **The Transformation** — Specific before/after examples
5. **The Results** — Metrics with honest assessment
6. **The Limitations** — What AI can't replace
7. **The Transferable Practices** — What readers can adopt

---

## KEY NARRATIVE GOAL

This isn't "AI is magic." It's "Here's how I systematically integrated AI into my workflow, measured the results, and developed judgment about when to use it."

The credibility comes from:
- Specific metrics, not vague claims
- Acknowledging limitations
- Showing the learning process, not just the outcome
- Demonstrating that the skill is replicable, not luck

---

## SUPPORTING EVIDENCE TO GATHER

- Git commit history showing feature delivery pace
- Sprint/project records with timelines
- Specific examples of AI-assisted code vs. what you would have written
- Time tracking data if available
- Any quality metrics (bug counts, review cycles)
