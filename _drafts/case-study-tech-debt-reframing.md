## CASE STUDY EXPANSION PLAN

**Original Post:** `_posts/2025-11-11-tech-debt-is-a-self-fulfilling-prophecy.md`

**What's Already Strong:**
- Clear problem diagnosis (why "tech debt" language fails)
- Practical reframing: Corrections, Optimizations, Re-Alignments
- Good examples of each category

**What's Missing:**
The reframing is compelling but unproven. Did it actually change how work got prioritized?

---

## ELEMENTS TO ADD

### 1. The Before State

- How was technical work communicated before the reframing?
- What language was used? ("Tech debt," "cleanup," "refactoring"?)
- How did stakeholders respond to these requests?
- What was the prioritization outcome? (Always deferred?)
- Specific examples of work that was deprioritized using old language

### 2. The Transition

- How did you introduce the new terminology?
- What resistance did you encounter?
- Who needed convincing? (Team? Leadership? Product?)
- How long did it take for the new language to stick?

### 3. The After State

Document specific submissions using new framework:

**Correction Example:**
- What was the issue?
- How was it framed as a Correction?
- What was the stakeholder response?
- Was it prioritized? Why or why not?

**Optimization Example:**
- What was the opportunity?
- How was it framed with business value?
- What metrics supported the case?
- Outcome?

**Re-Alignment Example:**
- What strategic shift required technical changes?
- How was it connected to business strategy?
- How did framing affect prioritization?

### 4. The Results

- Did prioritization actually change?
- What percentage of technical work got approved before vs. after?
- Did stakeholder perception of engineering change?
- Any pushback or unintended consequences?

## STRUCTURE FOR EXPANDED VERSION

1. **The Graveyard of Good Ideas** — Technical improvements that died as "tech debt"
2. **The Language Experiment** — Introducing Corrections/Optimizations/Re-Alignments
3. **The Resistance** — Who pushed back and why
4. **The Shift** — When stakeholders started responding differently
5. **The Evidence** — Specific wins using new framing
6. **The Honest Assessment** — What changed vs. what didn't

---

## KEY NARRATIVE GOAL

Transform from "here's a better way to communicate" to "here's proof that communication framing changes outcomes."

The credibility comes from:
- Showing the same type of work getting different treatment based on framing
- Acknowledging that framing alone doesn't guarantee approval
- Demonstrating that the approach is repeatable, not situational
