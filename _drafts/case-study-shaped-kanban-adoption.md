## ELEMENTS TO ADD

### 1. The Before State (Sprint-Based)

- What methodology was the team using? (Scrum? SAFe?)
- What were the pain points?
- Specific examples of work fragmented across sprints
- Features that sat incomplete at sprint boundaries
- Metrics: velocity variation, lead time, predictability

### 2. The Transition

- How did you propose the change?
- What resistance emerged? From whom?
- How did you handle the "but we've always done sprints" objection?
- What was the transition plan? (Cold turkey or gradual?)
- How long before it felt natural?

### 3. Specific Feature Examples

Document 2-3 features through shaped kanban:

**Feature A:**
- Scope and shaping process
- How long did it take?
- Did it hit any circuit breakers?
- How did completion compare to sprint-based estimates?

**Feature B:**
- A feature that would have been fragmented across sprints
- How shaped kanban handled it differently
- Outcome

**Feature C (if applicable):**
- A feature that hit the circuit breaker
- What happened? Rescope? Kill?
- How was that decision made?

### 4. Multi-Team Coordination

If applicable:
- How did shaped kanban handle dependencies?
- Real example of cross-team coordination
- What worked vs. what was harder than expected?

### 5. The Results

- Lead time: before vs. after
- Predictability: did teams get better at shaping estimates?
- Feature completion: fewer half-done features?
- Team satisfaction: how did developers feel about the change?
- Stakeholder perception: did product/leadership notice a difference?

4. **The First Feature** — A concrete example through the new process
5. **The Circuit Breaker Test** — When something didn't fit
6. **The Metrics** — Before/after comparison
7. **The Honest Assessment** — What worked, what didn't, what surprised you

---

## KEY NARRATIVE GOAL

Transform from "here's a better methodology" to "here's what happened when a real team tried it."

The credibility comes from:
- Specific features with timelines
- Acknowledging what was harder than expected
- Showing the circuit breaker mechanism in action
- Demonstrating that it's adoptable, not just theoretical
